Privacy statement

On the-good.org, hetgoede.org and le-bien.org you can sign up to stay informed about the book and the movement that grows from it. Here you can read what we do with your data.

Who is responsible

GKW Floré BV, Kasseitje 86, 9080 Lochristi, Belgium, company number BE 0655.758.305. Questions about your data can be sent to info@hetgoede.org.

Which data

Your first and last name, your email address, your country and postcode, what you would like to do, what you write about your expertise and your reason, the language in which you sign up, where your sign-up came from and the date.

What for

To keep you informed about the book and to involve you in the movement that is growing around it. As soon as an organisation has been set up to carry the movement, we will transfer your data to it, so that it can keep you informed. You gave your consent for this when you signed up.

Who processes it

Sign-ups are stored in Brevo, a European email service. The website is hosted by Netlify. Untold, the publisher of the book, manages both on behalf of GKW Floré. We never sell your data and never pass it on to anyone else.

Cookies

This website only places cookies if you agree to it. In that case we load the Meta pixel of Meta Platforms Ireland. It measures whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram bring people to this site, and whether they sign up or order the book. Without your consent, the pixel is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time via ‘Cookies’ at the bottom of the page.

How long

For as long as you stay signed up. Every email has a link at the bottom to unsubscribe. After that, we delete your data.

Your rights

You can ask at any time which data we hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted, via info@hetgoede.org. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority.
